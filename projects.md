# Projects

A selection of engineering projects spanning radar signal processing, semiconductor multimedia systems, Linux graphics and academic research.

---

## Radar & Signal Processing

### Sliding Spotlight Synthetic Aperture Radar Processor

Development of a DSP-based processing architecture for high-resolution Synthetic Aperture Radar imaging.

Key areas included:

- Processor architecture and data-flow design
- DSP-based signal processing
- Memory and computational requirements
- Algorithm implementation and optimization
- Raw-data simulation and processing
- Image-quality analysis and verification

### Microwave Radar Imaging

Engineering work involving microwave radar imaging algorithms and digital signal-processing techniques for radar imaging applications.

### FPGA-Based Digital Chirp Generation

Implementation and characterization of digital chirp-generation techniques for radar signal-processing applications.

### High-Speed ADC Characterization

Evaluation and characterization of high-speed analog-to-digital conversion for radar signal-processing systems.
